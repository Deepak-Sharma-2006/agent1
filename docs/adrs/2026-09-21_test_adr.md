# ADR: Architecture Decision Record for BHEDAK: Sovereign Autonomous Threat Triangulation Platform

> **Status**: `APPROVED WITH HARDENING` | **Domain**: `Cyber` | **Date**: `2026-09-21`

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
Tor darknet forensic

## 2. Decision & 4-Moat Defensibility
1. **Data Ingestion Moat**: Raw Tor SOCKS5 multi-hop timing buffers and mempool transaction feeds captured at line rate.
2. **Algorithmic Moat**: Heterogeneous Temporal Graph Neural Networks computing circuit correlations in <42ms vs days of manual work.
3. **Statutory Moat**: Statutory compliance under Section 63 Bhartiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) for court-admissible electronic evidence.
4. **Economic Moat**: High-throughput parallel C++/Python graph pipeline executing 10M correlations at 0.0008/query vs0.12 commercial tools.

## 3. Cryptographic Anti-Tamper Specification
- **Merkle Chain**: SHA-256 parent-chained forensic evidence blocks signed with Ed25519; any bit alteration invalidates tree.
- **Constant Time**: Crypto timingSafeEqual comparisons across all node IDs and forensic tokens to defeat timing attacks.
- **Enclave Isolation**: De-anonymization heuristics strictly execute inside isolated enclave; client SOC HUD receives verified proofs only.
