# SIH Championship Slide 3 Visual Graph Flow & Technical Approach Finalization Plan

## Executive Summary
This implementation plan establishes the finalized, end-to-end **Visual Graph Workflow Architecture** for Slide 3 ("TECHNICAL APPROACH") of both **Project BHEDAK** and **Project CHAKRA**, strictly aligning with the authentic solution blueprints and the operator's latest directives:

1. **Exact Authentic RBAC Cadres**:
   - **Project BHEDAK**: Strictly integrates the **3 authentic NTRO Scientific Cadre roles** (Scientist 'D' Ingestion Operator, Scientist 'E' Forensic Examiner, Scientist 'G' Centre Director).
   - **Project CHAKRA**: Strictly integrates the **5 authentic MHA / I4C statutory roles** (Tier 1: Investigating Officer, Tier 2: Supervisory Sanction Officer, Tier 3: Digital Forensic Examiner, Tier 4: Cyber Threat Analyst, Tier 5: VASP Compliance Nodal Officer).
2. **Unified Single-Graph Workflow**:
   - RBAC roles and operational dashboard features are directly embedded in the single sequential/spatial graph flow connecting starting point, engines, role hand-offs, and final legal/statutory outcomes.
3. **Clean & Simple Graph Flow**:
   - Eliminates redundant device badges or framing icons (no mobile/tablet, phone, or WebGL HUD tags).
   - Focuses purely on clean, high-contrast graph nodes, directional connectors with explicit data labels, clear role badges, and readable operational tab pills.
4. **Dedicated Bottom Tech Stack Section (~20% Canvas)**:
   - Positioned in a clean, separate bottom section, providing full subsystem explanations, performance benchmarks, and colored technology chips without crowding the workflow graph (~70% Canvas).
5. **Legible Typography & High Visual Contrast**:
   - Large, bold fonts across all components (13px - 17px), avoiding the small-text pitfall.
